# 02 — Channel Positioning

Positioning answers: **why should someone subscribe to *this* channel instead of watching one video and leaving?**

---

## A. The channel promise (one line)

Formula: **"I help [viewer] [achieve outcome] [through your unique method/angle]."**

Test it against these questions; rewrite until every answer is yes.
- [ ] Could a stranger tell in 3 seconds whether this channel is for them?
- [ ] Does it name a *result*, not a topic?
- [ ] Is there something in it that a competitor couldn't say?

This line goes in: channel banner, About section (first line), channel trailer, end of every video ("If you want to [outcome], subscribe").

## B. Positioning statement (internal)

> For **[viewer]** who **[pain/desire]**, **[Channel]** is the **[category]** that **[key benefit]**. Unlike **[main alternative]**, we **[differentiator]**.

## C. Pick your positioning angle (choose 1 primary)

| Angle | You win by… | Good when… |
|---|---|---|
| **The Guide** | Being the clearest teacher | Topic is confusing; existing content is too advanced |
| **The Journey** | Documenting your real progress | You're a beginner; viewers are one step behind you |
| **The Experimenter** | Testing claims so viewers don't have to | Niche full of hype and contradictory advice |
| **The Curator** | Filtering the noise into "only what matters" | Niche is overwhelming |
| **The Insider** | Access, data or experience others lack | You work in the industry |
| **The Contrarian** | Calling out bad advice with evidence | Niche is saturated with sameness |
| **The Specialist** | Going 10× deeper on a sub-topic | Big channels cover it superficially |

## D. Channel identity checklist

- [ ] **Name:** easy to say, easy to spell, suggests the promise or is your name. Check that the handle is available.
- [ ] **Profile picture:** face (on-camera) or simple high-contrast mark; readable at 40px.
- [ ] **Banner:** promise line + upload schedule ("New videos every Tuesday"); key content inside the 1546×423 safe area.
- [ ] **About:** promise → who it's for → what you'll get → upload day → business email.
- [ ] **Channel trailer** (for non-subscribers, 45–90s): hook with the viewer's pain → promise → 3 things they'll learn → proof → "subscribe if…".
- [ ] **Featured sections:** best-performing playlist first, then pillars as playlists.
- [ ] **Visual system:** 2 brand colors + 1 accent, 1 heavy font for thumbnails, consistent thumbnail layout.
- [ ] **Verbal system:** recurring intro line (≤5s, *after* the hook), sign-off, series names.

## E. Positioning sanity checks after 20 videos

- Look at **Audience → "Other channels your audience watches"**. Are they the channels you expected? If not, your packaging is attracting a different viewer than your content serves.
- Read 50 comments. Is the language what you'd expect from your viewer?
- What % of views come from **subscribers**? If < 10% after 20 videos, people watch but don't see a reason to come back, so the promise isn't clear.
