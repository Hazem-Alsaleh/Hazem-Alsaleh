# 03 — Content Pillars & Video Formats

Pillars decide **what** you talk about. Formats decide **how** you package it. A channel is the grid of pillars × formats.

---

## A. Content pillars (pick 3–4)

Each pillar must serve the channel promise and have **at least 20 video ideas** in it. If it doesn't, it's a single video, not a pillar.

Use this pillar mix:

| Pillar role | Purpose | Share of uploads | Traffic source it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search / Evergreen** | Answer specific questions people type | 40% | YouTube & Google search (compounds for years) |
| **Browse / Big idea** | Broad-appeal topics with curiosity packaging | 30% | Home feed & Suggested (spikes) |
| **Trust / Depth** | Story, behind the scenes, opinions, case studies | 20% | Subscribers (turns viewers into fans) |
| **Conversion** | Topics adjacent to what you'll sell | 10% | Monetization (leads, affiliates, products) |

## B. The 5 repeatable formats

Repeatable formats mean faster production, recognizable packaging, and binge-able series. Choose 5 from this list and **commit to them for 90 days**.

| Format | Structure | Length | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Tutorial** | Result first → steps → common mistakes → recap | 8–15 min | Search |
| **The List** | "7 X that Y", ranked or sorted by surprise | 8–14 min | Browse + search |
| **The Challenge / Experiment** | Stakes → attempt → setbacks → result → lesson | 10–20 min | Browse |
| **The Breakdown** | "Why X happened / How X works" with visuals | 10–18 min | Browse, high RPM niches |
| **The Comparison** | X vs Y → criteria → winner for whom | 8–15 min | Search + affiliate |
| **The Mistakes** | "Stop doing X" / "N mistakes beginners make" | 8–12 min | Browse + search |
| **The Journey Log** | "Day 1 to Day 30 of…", progress update | 8–15 min | Trust + series |
| **The Case Study** | Real example dissected, numbers shown | 10–20 min | Trust + conversion |
| **The Reaction / Review** | Critique with original analysis (must add substantial value) | 10–15 min | Browse |
| **The Short** | 1 idea, hook in <1s, payoff, loop | 20–50 s | Discovery + subs |

## C. Shorts strategy (the subscriber engine)

Shorts make subscribers cheaply but bring little watch time. Use them deliberately:
- **Clip Shorts:** the best 30–45s moment from each long-form video, recut vertically with a new hook. Use "Related video" to link to the full video.
- **Native Shorts:** one quick tip/myth/before-after per Short, made in the same pillars.
- **Teaser Shorts:** pose the question; long-form answers it.
- Never post Shorts on a *different* topic than your long-form. Those subscribers won't watch your long-form, and that hurts your channel signals.

## D. Idea bank system

Keep **one** spreadsheet or Notion database. Never start a week with zero ideas.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Working title | Write it like the final title |
| Pillar / Format | |
| Source | Outlier competitor video, comment, search autocomplete, own experience |
| Proof of demand | Link to outlier / search volume / comment count |
| Packaging score (1–10) | Would *you* click? Be harsh. |
| Effort (S/M/L) | |
| Status | Idea → Packaged → Scripted → Filmed → Edited → Scheduled → Published |

**Weekly input quota:** add 10 ideas/week. Only ideas scoring **≥ 7 on packaging** move to "Packaged".

### Six idea sources, ranked by hit rate
1. Outliers from small channels in your niche (3×+ their average)
2. Outliers from adjacent niches, adapted to yours
3. Questions in comments (yours and competitors')
4. YouTube autocomplete + "People also search"
5. Your own analytics: what's getting the most search impressions?
6. Your experience: mistakes, results, stories (best for trust content)
