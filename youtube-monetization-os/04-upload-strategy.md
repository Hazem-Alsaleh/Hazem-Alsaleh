# 04 — Upload Strategy

## A. Choose your cadence tier (from your *realistic* weekly hours)

| Tier | Weekly hours | Long-form | Shorts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimum viable** | 5–7 | 1 every 2 weeks | 3/week | Slow but survivable. Lean hard on search content. |
| **Standard** | 8–12 | 1/week | 3–5/week | Recommended default. |
| **Aggressive** | 15–25 | 2/week | 5–7/week | Fastest learning, highest burnout risk. |
| **Full-time** | 30+ | 2–3/week | Daily | Only with a repeatable format and/or an editor. |

> **Consistency > frequency.** Weekly for 52 weeks beats 3×/week for 6 weeks then silence. Choose the tier you could keep up during your worst month of the year.

**Quality ratchet:** Start one tier *lower* than you think. Raise the tier only after 4 consecutive weeks on schedule.

## B. Upload timing

- Publish **2–3 hours before** your audience's peak (Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube"). Before you have data, publish at 14:00–16:00 in your main audience's time zone.
- Pick fixed days so viewers and *you* build a habit. Put the schedule in the banner.
- **Shorts:** spread them out. Don't publish a Short within ~6 hours of a long-form upload; let the long-form get its initial push.

## C. Release ladder (first 12 weeks)

| Weeks | Focus | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Search-led** tutorials/comparisons on specific questions | Search traffic doesn't need subscribers; it gets your first viewers |
| 5–8 | Add **browse-style** videos on your best-performing search topics | Use data to bet on bigger topics |
| 9–12 | Launch **Series #1** around your best format | Series → binge sessions → watch hours |

## D. Pre-publish checklist (every long-form video)

**Packaging**
- [ ] Title ≤ 60 characters, curiosity or clear outcome, main keyword in the first half
- [ ] Thumbnail: 1 focal point, ≤ 4 words, readable on a phone at 20% size, different from the title (they *complement* each other)
- [ ] 2–3 thumbnail variants uploaded to **Test & Compare**
- [ ] First frame + first line deliver what the title/thumbnail promised

**Metadata**
- [ ] Description: line 1–2 restate the promise with keywords (shows in search); then summary, timestamps/chapters, links, related videos
- [ ] Chapters (00:00 first, ≥ 3 chapters, ≥ 10s each). Name them like search queries.
- [ ] 3–5 tags (low impact, but harmless: main keyword + misspellings)
- [ ] Added to 1–2 playlists
- [ ] End screen → **one** best-next video (a specific video beats "best for viewer" when you have a clear binge path) + subscribe
- [ ] Pinned comment: a question that invites replies, or the next-step link
- [ ] Captions checked (auto-captions corrected for key terms)
- [ ] Correct altered/synthetic content disclosure if you used realistic AI generation
- [ ] Paid-promotion box ticked if anything was sponsored or gifted

**Distribution (first 24h)**
- [ ] Clip Short scheduled for 24–48h later, linked to the video
- [ ] Community post (poll or image) 24h after
- [ ] Shared to email list / 1–2 communities where it genuinely helps (no spam)
- [ ] Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours

## E. Title formulas that work across niches

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| How to [result] [without pain] | How to Learn React Without Tutorial Hell |
| I tried [X] for [time]. Here's what happened | I Coded Every Day for 100 Days. Here's What Happened |
| [Number] [things] I wish I knew before [X] | 7 Things I Wish I Knew Before Learning to Code |
| Stop [common action] (do this instead) | Stop Watching Coding Tutorials (Do This Instead) |
| Why [surprising claim] | Why Most Beginner Portfolios Get Ignored |
| [X] vs [Y]: which is actually better for [person]? | React vs Vue: Which Should Beginners Learn in 2027? |
| The [adjective] way to [result] | The Fastest Way to Build a Portfolio That Gets Interviews |
| I [did hard thing] so you don't have to | I Applied to 100 Dev Jobs So You Don't Have To |
