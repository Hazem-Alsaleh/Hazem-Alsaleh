# 06 — Growth Systems

Five systems working together. Each one has an owner (you), a cadence, and a metric.

```
      ┌────────────── Idea Engine ──────────────┐
      │ (outliers + comments + search + data)   │
      ▼                                         │
 Search Engine ─► viewers ─► Series Engine ─► binge ─► subscribers
      ▲                          │                      │
      │                          ▼                      ▼
 Analytics Loop ◄────────── Shorts Engine        Community & Collab Engine
```

---

## 1. Search Engine (compounding traffic)

**Goal:** 30–50% of long-form views from YouTube/Google search by month 6.

- Target questions people type, phrased the way they type them ("how to…", "X vs Y", "best X for Y", "X for beginners").
- Put the exact phrase in the title, the first line of the description, and spoken in the first 30 seconds (YouTube transcribes speech).
- Answer **better** than the current #1: more current, clearer, with a visual demo, or for a more specific person.
- **Content clusters:** 1 pillar video ("Complete guide to X") + 5–8 sub-videos linking to and from it via cards, end screens and playlists.
- **Metric:** Traffic source → YouTube search % and top search terms. Every month, make a video on the top search term you rank for but didn't target directly.

## 2. Series Engine (watch-hour machine)

**Goal:** Turn one-off views into multi-video sessions.

- Once a format or topic hits (CTR and AVD above your average), make it a **named series** with consistent packaging ("100 Days of…", "Beginner Mistakes Ep. 3", "Rebuilding X").
- Build a playlist for each series. Every episode's end screen points to the next episode.
- Series give returning viewers a reason to subscribe ("I want episode 4").
- **Metric:** Views per unique viewer, and returning viewers %.

## 3. Shorts Engine (subscriber machine)

**Goal:** Shorts subscribers who also watch long-form.

- 3–5 Shorts/week in your pillars. Half clipped from long-form, half native.
- Hook in the first frame: text overlay + a motion or statement. No logos, no intros.
- Design the end to loop back into the start where possible.
- Link every clipped Short to its long-form ("Related video").
- **Metric:** Subscribers gained per 1,000 Shorts views (aim for > 1), and whether Shorts viewers show up on your long-form analytics.

## 4. Community & Collab Engine (trust + borrowed audiences)

**Community (every week)**
- Reply to **every** comment for the first year. Heart the good ones. Pin a question.
- Turn the best comments into videos ("You asked: …"), and credit the commenter.
- 1–2 Community posts/week (polls about next video topics are the easiest win: they produce engagement *and* idea validation).
- Start an email list or Discord early (month 2–3). You own this audience; YouTube can change the algorithm.

**Collabs (monthly from month 4)**
- Target creators at **0.5×–3× your size** in an adjacent niche.
- Pitch a specific video idea that's good for *their* audience, not a generic "let's collab".
- Formats: guest segment, joint challenge, "I asked an expert", reaction to each other's content, podcast-style conversation.
- **Metric:** Subscribers gained per collab; new returning viewers.

**Off-platform amplification (light-touch)**
- Answer questions on Reddit/forums/LinkedIn/X with genuinely helpful written answers; link the video only when it's the best answer and the community allows it.
- Repurpose each long-form into 1 carousel/thread on your best social platform.

## 5. Analytics Loop (the steering wheel)

**Weekly (20 minutes)** — see [templates/weekly-review.md](templates/weekly-review.md)
- For each new video: impressions, CTR, AVD, % viewed, subs gained, top traffic source.
- One decision: *keep / tweak packaging / kill this format*.

**Monthly (60 minutes)**
- Rank all videos by **views per day since publish** and **subs per 1,000 views**.
- Top 20% → make more like these (same topic, new angle, series).
- Bottom 20% → why? Packaging (low CTR) or content (low retention)?
- Swap thumbnails/titles on videos with good retention but low CTR. Old videos can be revived.
- Check: Audience → "Content your audience watches" → feed the idea bank.

**Quarterly (half day)**
- Re-run the niche score and positioning check (modules 01–02).
- Kill one format, introduce one new one.
- Update the monetization plan with real numbers (module 07).

### The diagnosis tree

```
Video underperformed?
├─ Impressions low?  → topic too narrow, or no audience signal yet (keep publishing)
├─ CTR low?          → packaging problem → new title/thumbnail (Test & Compare)
├─ CTR ok, AVD low?  → content/hook problem → watch retention graph
├─ AVD ok, few subs? → no clear reason to return → stronger promise/series/CTA
└─ All ok, no spread → topic appeal too small → go broader on the same idea
```
