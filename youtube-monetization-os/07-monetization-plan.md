# 07 — Monetization Plan

AdSense is the *last* revenue stream most small channels unlock, and rarely the biggest. Build the stack in parallel.

---

## A. YPP math: what do *your* numbers need to be?

**4,000 watch hours = 240,000 minutes of long-form watch time in a rolling 12 months.**

```
Long-form views needed (12 months) = 240,000 ÷ Average View Duration (minutes)
```

| AVD | Views needed / 12 mo | Per week (52 wks) | Per video @ 1 video/wk | Per video @ 2/wk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 min | 120,000 | 2,308 | ~2,300 | ~1,150 |
| 3 min | 80,000 | 1,538 | ~1,540 | ~770 |
| **4 min** | **60,000** | **1,154** | **~1,150** | **~580** |
| 5 min | 48,000 | 923 | ~920 | ~460 |
| 6 min | 40,000 | 769 | ~770 | ~385 |
| 8 min | 30,000 | 577 | ~580 | ~290 |

**What this tells you**
- Raising AVD from 3 → 5 minutes cuts the views you need by **40%**. That's why longer *and* well-retained videos (10–20 min) are the efficient path to watch hours.
- Views on old videos count. A library of 50 search videos each getting 30 views/day = 1,500 views/day = the target, every day, without a new upload. **Search content is a watch-hour annuity.**
- Live streams and premieres count as long-form public watch time (but deleted or private videos stop counting).

**Subscriber math:** typical small channels convert ~0.5–2% of long-form viewers and ~0.05–0.3% of Shorts viewers into subscribers. 1,000 subs ≈ 50k–200k combined views with good conversion.

## B. The revenue stack (by when you can start)

| Stream | Start when | Typical effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate links** | Video 1 | Low | Tools/gear/courses you actually use. Disclose clearly. Comparison and "best X" videos convert best. |
| **Lead magnet → email list** | ~Month 2 | Low–Med | Free checklist/template/guide. The list is the asset everything else sells through. |
| **Services / consulting / freelance** | ~200–500 subs | Med | Highest $/viewer early on. One client can out-earn a year of AdSense. |
| **Digital product** (template, mini-course, ebook, preset pack) | ~500–1,000 subs + email list | Med–High | Build what comments repeatedly ask for. Pre-sell before building. |
| **Expanded YPP**: memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping | 500 subs + 3k hrs | Low | Members' perks: early access, Discord, behind the scenes, monthly Q&A. |
| **Full YPP**: ads | 1,000 subs + 4k hrs | Low | RPM varies hugely by niche and audience country (roughly $1–$15+). |
| **Sponsorships / brand deals** | ~5k–10k subs (earlier in B2B/high-value niches) | Med | Build a 1-page media kit; pitch brands whose products you already use. |
| **Community/cohort/course** | Engaged audience | High | Highest income ceiling; requires trust built over many videos. |

### Revenue planning formula
```
Monthly AdSense ≈ (monthly monetized long-form views ÷ 1,000) × RPM
Monthly affiliate ≈ views × link CTR (0.5–2%) × conversion (2–10%) × commission
Monthly product ≈ email subs × monthly offer exposure × conversion (1–3%) × price
```

Example (illustrative): 30k monthly views at a $4 RPM ≈ $120 in AdSense. The same 30k views with 1% clicking an affiliate link, 5% of those buying, at $30 commission ≈ $450.

## C. Monetization guardrails (protect your YPP approval)

- **Original value:** YPP reviewers check your main theme, most-viewed videos, newest videos and watch-time leaders. Mass-produced, templated, low-effort or reused content (re-uploads, compilations without commentary, unedited AI voice over stock footage) gets rejected under the inauthentic/reused content policies.
- **AI use:** AI tools for scripting, editing, voice or visuals are fine when you add real value. Disclose realistic synthetic content using the "altered or synthetic content" setting.
- **Music & footage:** use only YouTube Audio Library, licensed libraries, or your own. Copyright claims can block monetization of individual videos.
- **Advertiser-friendly guidelines:** heavy profanity in the first 7 seconds, shocking thumbnails, or sensitive topics can limit ads ("yellow icon").
- **Sponsorships:** always tick "includes paid promotion" and disclose verbally.
- **Never buy subscribers, views or watch hours.** It violates policy, gets removed, and wrecks your analytics signals.

## D. Pre-YPP application checklist
- [ ] 2-Step Verification on the Google account
- [ ] AdSense account ready (one per person)
- [ ] Clear channel theme. Delete or make private any off-topic, low-quality early experiments that could confuse a reviewer (but remember private videos stop counting toward watch hours. Check that you'll still clear 4,000.)
- [ ] Your 10 most-viewed and 10 newest videos show clear original contribution
- [ ] No active strikes
- [ ] Apply the day you're eligible: review can take a month or more, and you can reapply 30 days after a rejection (fix the stated reason first)
