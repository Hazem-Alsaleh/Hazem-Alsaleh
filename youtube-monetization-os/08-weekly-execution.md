# 08 — Weekly Execution Schedule

The system runs on a **2-week production pipeline**: this week you publish what you filmed last week, while you package and script next week's video. Your publish date never depends on this week going well.

```
Week N:    [Package N+2]  [Script N+1]  [Film N+1]  [Edit N+1]  [Publish N]
```

---

## A. Standard tier (≈ 10 hrs/week) — 1 long-form + 3–4 Shorts

| Day | Block | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 🧠 Plan | Weekly review (template) → pick next video from idea bank → write 5 titles + sketch 3 thumbnails → **package-test** (would you click?) | 1.5 h |
| **Tue** | ✍️ Script | Script/outline next video using the retention skeleton. Write hook word-for-word. Add 10 ideas to the bank. | 1.5 h |
| **Wed** | 🎥 Film | Batch-film long-form + 3–4 native Shorts in one session | 2 h |
| **Thu** | ✂️ Edit | Edit long-form (rough cut → tight cut) | 2 h |
| **Fri** | 🎨 Finish | Thumbnail variants, captions, chapters, description, end screen; cut 2 clip-Shorts; **schedule everything** | 1.5 h |
| **Sat** | 🚀 Publish + engage | Publish long-form (scheduled) → reply to all comments in the first 2 hrs → community post | 1 h |
| **Sun** | 🔋 Off | Rest. Optional: watch 2 competitor outliers and take notes. | — |

Shorts schedule: Mon, Wed, Fri (+ Sun optional), published from the batch.

## B. Minimum viable tier (≈ 6 hrs/week) — long-form every 2 weeks + 3 Shorts

- **Week A:** Plan + Script (2h), Film (2h), Shorts batch (1h), engage (1h)
- **Week B:** Edit (3h), Finish + publish (1.5h), engage + review (1.5h)

## C. Aggressive tier (≈ 20 hrs/week) — 2 long-form + daily Shorts

Run the Standard week twice, with filming batched for **both** long-form videos on one day and editing on two days. If you can afford an editor, outsource editing first; it's the most time-consuming task and the easiest to delegate.

---

## D. Daily non-negotiables (15 min)
- Reply to new comments
- Add 2 ideas to the idea bank
- Check yesterday's video for one number (CTR or AVD) and nothing else. Daily metric-watching is anxiety, not strategy.

## E. 90-day sprint plan

| Week | Milestone | Key action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Setup | Complete modules 01–02. Channel art, About, 30 ideas banked, 3 videos packaged |
| 1 | Launch | Publish video #1 + 3 Shorts. Don't announce a "launch", just publish. |
| 2–3 | Rhythm | Hit schedule both weeks. Build the habit; don't judge results yet. |
| 4 | **Review #1** | First monthly review. Which title/thumbnail style got the best CTR? |
| 5–6 | Iterate | Re-package your 2 lowest-CTR videos. Try 1 new format. |
| 7–8 | Lean in | Make a follow-up to your best video. Start your email list/lead magnet. |
| 9 | Signal check | Any video with CTR & AVD above average? That's your series seed. |
| 10–11 | Series #1 | Launch the named series + playlist + end-screen chain |
| 12 | **Quarter review** | Re-score the niche, kill the weakest format, set next 90-day targets |
| 13 | Rest week | Publish a pre-made video; no filming. Prevents burnout. |

### Realistic 90-day targets (standard tier)
- 12 long-form + 40 Shorts published (the only target fully within your control: **hit this one**)
- 50–300 subscribers
- 200–800 watch hours
- 1 video that clearly outperformed (your signal)

## F. Weekly scorecard (aim for 6/7 every week)

| # | Leading indicator (what you control) | ✔ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Long-form published on schedule | |
| 2 | Shorts target hit | |
| 3 | Next video packaged before filming | |
| 4 | Video after next scripted (pipeline is 2 weeks ahead) | |
| 5 | Weekly review done + one decision logged | |
| 6 | All comments replied to | |
| 7 | 10 ideas added to the bank | |

**Lagging indicators** (subs, views, watch hours) are the result of the leading ones. Track them weekly, but judge yourself on the leading ones. That's how you get through months 2–4.

## G. Burnout & plateau protocol

- **Missed a week?** Don't make it up. Just publish the next one. Never post "sorry I've been gone" videos.
- **Bank buffer:** keep 1–2 finished videos in reserve for sick weeks and holidays.
- **Every 12th week = lighter week** (publish from buffer, no filming).
- **Plateau for 6+ weeks?** Run the diagnosis tree (module 06). Usually the fix is *packaging* or a *broader topic*, not "post more".
