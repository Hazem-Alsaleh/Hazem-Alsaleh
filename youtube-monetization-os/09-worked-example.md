## 1. Diagnosis

| React tutorials | 5 | 4 | 3 | 6 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 27 ❌ |

**Why the winner wins:** A beginner can't out-teach senior-dev channels on React, but *can* be the most relatable "one step ahead" guide. The edge is **authenticity + recency**: what learning web dev is actually like right now, including AI tools and the current job market.

**Viewer:** "Sam", 19–30, self-teaching or in a bootcamp, stuck between tutorials and real projects, anxious about whether AI will make junior roles disappear. Uses phrases like "tutorial hell", "I don't know what to build", "is it too late to learn coding".

**Competitor gap:** Big channels are taught by seniors (can feel distant); journey channels often stop at "I got a job" without showing the *how*. Gap: **project-based, honest, job-focused journey with replicable steps.**

## 2. Positioning
- **Promise:** "I help self-taught developers escape tutorial hell by building real projects, and show exactly what it takes to get hired."
- **Angle:** The Journey (primary) + The Experimenter (secondary)
- **Channel name ideas:** "Hazem Builds", "Build With Hazem", "Hazem Codes in Public"
- **Banner:** "Real projects. No tutorial hell. New video every Saturday."

## 4. Five formats (90-day commitment)
1. **Build-along** (15–25 min): finished project shown first → build → bugs I hit → deploy
2. **"I tried X for N days"** experiment (10–14 min)
3. **Mistakes/Stop doing** list (8–12 min)
4. **Monthly honest update** with numbers (10 min): becomes the series
5. **Shorts:** 1 CSS/JS trick, 1 "bug of the week", 1 clip from the build-along

## 6. Monetization path
- **Day 1:** affiliate links to hosting/domain/course/tools you actually use, in build-along descriptions
- **Month 2:** lead magnet: "30 Beginner Project Ideas + Starter Repos" → email list
- **Month 3–4:** offer simple website builds to small local businesses; showcase them in videos (with client permission)
- **Month 6+:** sell the project source code + extended walkthroughs as a template pack
- **YPP math:** build-alongs are long, so a 6-min AVD is achievable → ~40k long-form views/year → **~770 views/week**. Search-led build-alongs get there via an evergreen library.
