# 11 — Viral Video Creation Engine

A repeatable pipeline from idea to final script, built to maximize **click-through rate** and **retention**. [Module 05](05-retention-framework.md) has the retention fundamentals; this module is the production engine with worked examples and full scripts.

> "Viral" here means *outperforming your own baseline by 3×+*. You can't force virality. You *can* make every video eligible for it.

---

## A. The engine: 7 stages

```
1. CONCEPT  →  2. ANGLE  →  3. PACKAGE  →  4. OPENING  →  5. STRUCTURE  →  6. SCRIPT  →  7. QC
   (want)       (twist)      (click)        (stay)          (keep)          (words)       (ship)
```

### 1. Concept: does the viewer already want it?
A concept is viral-eligible when it hits **at least 2** of these 6 triggers:

| Trigger | Viewer feeling | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity gap** | "I need to know what happened" | "I let AI build my portfolio for a week" |
| **Desire / result** | "I want that" | "The portfolio that got me 4 interviews" |
| **Fear / loss** | "Am I doing it wrong?" | "Stop doing this if you're learning to code" |
| **Identity** | "That's me!" | "If you're stuck in tutorial hell, watch this" |
| **Challenge / stakes** | "Can they do it?" | "Building and selling a website in 48 hours" |
| **Controversy / contrarian** | "Wait, really?" | "Learning React first is a mistake" |

### 2. Angle: the twist that makes it yours
Take a proven topic and apply one angle modifier:
- **Time pressure:** "in 24 hours", "in 7 days"
- **Constraint:** "with $0", "only using AI", "without tutorials"
- **Comparison:** "beginner vs senior builds the same app"
- **Numbers/proof:** "I tracked every hour for 30 days"
- **Reverse:** "How to fail at learning to code (so you don't)"
- **Personal stakes:** "If this doesn't work, I quit"

### 3. Package: title + thumbnail as a pair
- Write **20 titles**, pick 3, then test them with Test & Compare (thumbnail) and with a quick poll of 3 people in your target audience.
- The title states the *topic/claim*; the thumbnail shows the *emotion/result*. Never write the title text on the thumbnail.
- **Thumbnail concept formula:** `[Subject] + [Emotion or transformation] + [≤ 4 words that add tension]`

## D. Opening scene blueprints

| Blueprint | Shot list (0–30s) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **The Reveal** | 0–3s: the finished result on screen → 3–10s: "here's how to build it" + why it matters → 10–30s: what they'll learn + one teaser | Tutorials, build-alongs |
| **The Cold Open** | 0–5s: the most dramatic moment (bug, rejection, deadline) → 5–15s: "but let's back up" → 15–30s: the stakes and the goal | Challenges, stories |
| **The Contrast** | 0–5s: bad vs good side by side → 5–20s: "the difference is X things" → 20–30s: "the last one is the one nobody fixes" | Mistakes, comparisons |
| **The Question** | 0–5s: the big question to camera → 5–20s: why the usual answer is wrong → 20–30s: how you'll answer it (test/data) | Debates, experiments |
| **The Countdown** | 0–3s: the timer starts → 3–15s: rules + stakes → 15–30s: first action, in motion | Speedruns, challenges |

**Never in the first 30s:** a logo intro, "hey guys welcome back", a channel plug, a sponsor read, or "before we start…".

## E. Storytelling structures

**1. Problem → Agitate → Solve (PAS)**: tutorials, mistakes
Problem stated → why it hurts / what it costs → solution step by step → proof it worked.

**2. The Challenge Arc**: experiments, speedruns
Goal + stakes → plan → early win → **major setback (midpoint)** → adaptation → climax (did it work?) → lesson.

**3. The Countdown List**: "N things" videos
Hook teases #1 → order from *good to best* (never best first) → re-hook between items ("but #3 is the one that changed everything") → best item last.

**4. The Investigation**: "why X" breakdowns
Mystery → clue 1 → wrong theory → clue 2 → twist → answer → implication for the viewer.

**5. Before / After Transformation**: rebuilds, glow-ups
Show the "after" briefly → back to "before" → process with struggles → full reveal → what to steal.

**6. The But/Therefore chain** (use inside every structure)
Every section connects with **"but"** (conflict) or **"therefore"** (consequence), never "and then".

## F. Retention technique stack (apply in every script)

| Technique | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| Open loops | Hook + every 2–3 min | "Remember the bug from the start? It's coming back." |
| Re-hooks | Before every section change | "This next part is where most people quit." |
| Visual change | Every 5–15s (long), every 1–3s (Shorts) | Zoom, B-roll, screen ↔ face, text pop-up |
| Progress markers | Throughout | "Step 2 of 4", day counters, timers |
| Stakes reminders | Midpoint | "If this doesn't work, the whole project fails." |
| Payoff ladder | Each section | A small win in every section; the biggest at the end |
| Pattern breaks | Mid-video | A joke, blooper, unexpected test, sound effect |
| Viewer involvement | 2–3 times | "Pause and guess what's wrong", "comment your answer" |
| Bridge ending | Last 20s | Send them to a specific next video, not a generic outro |

## G. CTA library (one per video, placed after value)

## H. Full script #1 — Long-form (≈ 10 min)

**Title:** I Banned Tutorials for 30 Days. Here's What Happened
**Thumbnail:** face (determined) + YouTube play button crossed out in red + "DAY 30"
**Structure:** Challenge Arc · **Opening:** Cold Open

```
[0:00 — COLD OPEN]
(Screen: a broken app, red errors everywhere. Face cam, frustrated.)
"Day 9. Nothing works, and the rule is I'm not allowed to look up a single tutorial."

[0:05 — STAKES]
(Cut to: calendar, 30 days, a big red X over a tutorial thumbnail.)
"I've done four coding courses. Hundreds of hours. And when I open an empty file,
I freeze. That's tutorial hell, and if you're watching this, you've probably felt it too.
So I gave myself one rule for 30 days: no tutorials. Only documentation, my own brain,
and building real things."

[0:20 — PROMISE / OPEN LOOP]
"By day 30 I'd built something I never thought I could, and I found one method that
works better than any course I've paid for. I'll show you exactly what it is.
But first, week one nearly ended the whole experiment."

[0:35 — WEEK 1: THE STRUGGLE]   (Progress marker: "WEEK 1/4")
"The plan: build a weather app. Should be easy, right? I'd built one in a tutorial before."
(Screen recording, sped-up struggle footage.)
"Day 1: two hours to fetch data from an API. In the tutorial, it took four minutes.
Here's what I didn't realise: following along and knowing how are completely different skills."
(Text on screen: "Following ≠ knowing")
"Pause here. If you've ever felt like you understood a tutorial and then couldn't
repeat it, comment 'same'. I want to see how many of us there are."

[2:00 — RE-HOOK]
"By day 5 I was ready to quit. Then I tried something different, and it's the method
I promised you at the start."

[2:10 — WEEK 2: THE METHOD]     ("WEEK 2/4")
"I call it Read → Break → Rebuild.
One: read the documentation for one tiny thing, like 'how does fetch work'.
Two: break it on purpose. Change things, see what errors you get.
Three: rebuild it from memory, without looking."
(Demo on screen, 60 seconds, fast cuts.)
"It's slower on day one. It's MUCH faster by day ten. Here's why it works: you're
practising recall instead of recognition..."

[4:00 — MIDPOINT SETBACK]       ("WEEK 3/4")
"Then week 3 happened."
(Cold-open footage again: errors.)
"Remember day 9 from the very beginning? This is it. My app worked on my laptop and
completely broke on my phone. And I couldn't watch a tutorial to fix it."
"Here's how I actually solved it..."  (debugging walkthrough; viewer-involvement:
"Guess what the bug is. Ten seconds.")

[6:30 — WEEK 4: THE BUILD]      ("WEEK 4/4")
"With the method working, I set a bigger goal: a full project, start to finish, alone."
(Montage + key moments. A small win shown every 30–40 seconds.)

[8:00 — CLIMAX / REVEAL]
"Day 30. Here's what I built."
(Full reveal of the finished app, smooth screen demo, music lift.)
"Thirty days ago, I couldn't do this without someone holding my hand."

[8:40 — LESSONS]
"Three things I learned:
One: tutorials aren't bad, but they're a starting point, not a method.
Two: documentation is scary for about a week, and then it's your superpower.
Three: getting stuck isn't failing. Getting stuck IS the learning."

[9:20 — CTA + BRIDGE]
"I put the Read → Break → Rebuild method and the 30 project ideas I used in a free
guide, linked in the description.
And if you're wondering how to turn a project like this into a portfolio that actually
gets replies, I tested that next. Watch this one."
(End screen: portfolio video + subscribe.)
```

**Why this script works**
- The cold open creates an **unresolved problem** (day 9) that pays off at minute 4. That's the open loop.
- Week markers act as a **progress bar**: viewers know where they are and how much remains.
- The **promised method** is delivered at ~2 min (early value builds trust), and the **finished project** is held for the end (the payoff).
- The viewer-involvement prompts add comments, which signal engagement.
- The bridge sends viewers to a specific next video, which extends the session.

## I. Full script #2 — Short (≈ 35 s)

**Title:** Stop putting this on your portfolio
**On-screen text frame 1:** "Recruiters skip this 👇"

```
[0.0s]  (Screen: portfolio with a to-do app, weather app, calculator.)
        "If your portfolio looks like this, it's getting skipped."
[2.5s]  (Zoom on "To-Do App".)
        "To-do, weather, calculator: recruiters see these 50 times a day."
[6s]    "Here's the fix: same skills, real problem."
[8s]    (Quick cuts, one per line, text on screen.)
        "To-do app → a booking tool for a local barber."
        "Weather app → a 'should I bike today?' app for your city."
        "Calculator → a freelance rate calculator."
[18s]   (Show one of them working.)
        "Same code. But now there's a story: who it's for and what problem it solves."
[25s]   "That's what gets you the interview."
[28s]   (Back to the first frame.) "So… does your portfolio look like this?"
        → loops back to the opening shot
```
**Why it works:** the first line triggers fear and identity; a concrete transformation follows; it ends with a question that loops back to the first frame, which boosts replays. Link it to video #19 (portfolio) as the related video.

## K. Virality QC checklist (score before filming, need ≥ 8/10)

- [ ] The concept hits 2+ triggers
- [ ] Title is ≤ 60 characters and creates a gap or promises a clear result
- [ ] Thumbnail reads in 1 second on a phone and adds to the title (doesn't repeat it)
- [ ] The first 5 seconds visually confirm the title/thumbnail
- [ ] Stakes are stated in the first 20s
- [ ] At least 2 open loops, each paid off
- [ ] Re-hook before every section change
- [ ] A visual change every 5–15 seconds
- [ ] The biggest payoff comes in the last 25% of the video
- [ ] Ends with a bridge to a specific video, not "bye"
