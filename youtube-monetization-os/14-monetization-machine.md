# 14 — Monetization Machine: The Creator Business System

[Module 07](07-monetization-plan.md) covers YPP math and guardrails. This module is the full creator business: every revenue stream, **when to switch it on**, how to run it, and how the streams feed each other.

> Numbers below are rough illustrative ranges. They vary enormously by niche, audience country, and engagement. Use them for planning shape, not promises.

---

## A. The stage-gated revenue map

Turn on a stream when you reach its stage, not before. Every stream you add too early takes time away from making videos.

| Stage | Audience | Switch ON | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S0 — Launch** | 0–100 subs | Affiliate links · Newsletter signup (lead magnet) | Learn what viewers need; collect emails from day 1 |
| **S1 — Traction** | 100–500 | Services/freelance (if relevant) · Simple digital product (≤ $30) | First $1 from your audience; validate demand |
| **S2 — Expanded YPP** | 500 subs + 3k hrs (or 3M Shorts views) | Memberships · Super Thanks/Chat · YouTube Shopping | Monetize superfans |
| **S3 — Full YPP** | 1k subs + 4k hrs (or 10M Shorts views) | AdSense · Brand outreach begins | Baseline income; media kit |
| **S4 — Authority** | 5k–25k | Sponsorships · Premium digital products · Paid community | Diversified income ≥ AdSense ×3 |
| **S5 — Business** | 25k–100k+ | Flagship course/cohort · Licensing · Speaking · Own product/SaaS | Scale; hire help |

**Order of priority:** email list → affiliate → services/product → YPP → sponsors → premium products. The email list comes first because you own it.

## B. Stream-by-stream playbooks

### 1. YouTube Partner Program preparation (S0–S3)
- Track the eligibility numbers monthly (module 07 table).
- From video 1, make content **that would pass review**: original, consistent theme, no reused clips, licensed music.
- 30 days before you expect to hit the threshold: audit your 10 newest and 10 most-viewed videos, set up AdSense + 2-Step Verification.
- Apply the day you're eligible; keep uploading on schedule during review.

### 2. AdSense strategy (S3+)
**Revenue ≈ monetized views ÷ 1,000 × RPM.** To raise it:
| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| **Topic RPM** | Finance, software, business, tech and education topics attract higher-paying advertisers. Lean your *conversion pillar* toward them. |
| **Length** | Videos **8+ minutes** can carry mid-roll ads. Place mid-rolls at natural pauses (manual placement often retains better than automatic). |
| **Watch time** | Longer AVD = more ad impressions per view |
| **Audience geography** | Viewers in high-ad-spend countries are worth more. Language and topic influence this. |
| **Seasonality** | Q4 (Oct–Dec) ad rates rise; January dips. Schedule your best videos for Q4. |
| **Ad suitability** | Avoid profanity in the first seconds, shocking thumbnails, and sensitive topics without context. Check for yellow icons. |
| **Shorts** | Shorts revenue share pays far less per view. Treat Shorts as subscriber acquisition, not income. |

### 3. Affiliate marketing (S0+)
- Only promote what you use. Make a **"My Tools"** page (link in every description).
- Best formats: comparisons, "best X for Y", tutorials that use the product, setup tours.
- Mention it **at the moment of need** in the video, not at the end.
- Use trackable links per video (UTM or affiliate sub-IDs) to learn which videos convert.
- **Disclose** in the description and verbally; tick paid promotion if you received anything of value.
- Example programs by type: hosting/domains, software/SaaS, online course platforms, Amazon Associates for gear.

### 4. Newsletter / email list (S0+): the foundation
- **Lead magnet:** a free checklist, template, starter code, or mini-guide directly tied to your most popular videos.
- **Cadence:** weekly or bi-weekly. New video + one exclusive insight + one recommendation.
- **Tools:** Beehiiv, ConvertKit (Kit), MailerLite: all have free starter tiers.
- **Monetization later:** your own products (main), affiliates, newsletter sponsorships (once you have ~1k+ engaged subscribers).
- **Target conversion:** 1–3% of video viewers in a strong lead-magnet video.

### 5. Services / consulting / freelancing (S1+)
- Often the **highest $ per viewer** for small channels. One client can earn more than a year of early AdSense.
- Make the work into content (with permission): case studies attract more clients.
- Add a "Work with me" link and an About-page line.

### 6. Digital products (S1 small → S4 premium)
| Tier | Example | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| Tripwire | Template, cheat sheet, preset, starter kit | $5–$30 |
| Core | E-book, Notion system, code bundle, mini-course | $30–$150 |
| Premium | Full course, toolkit + support | $150–$1,000+ |

**Validation process:**
1. Find a question asked repeatedly in comments.
2. Make a free video on it → offer a "go deeper" waitlist in the description.
3. **Pre-sell** to the waitlist at a launch discount before building.
4. Build with the first buyers' feedback.

Platforms: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip (simple products); Teachable, Podia, Kajabi (courses).

### 7. Courses & cohorts (S4–S5)
- Only after you've taught the topic many times on YouTube and know the common sticking points.
- **Cohort-based** first (live, smaller, higher price, easier to sell and improve) → **self-paced** later from the recordings.
- Structure around the viewer's transformation (module 01's dream outcome), not around topics.

### 8. Channel memberships (S2+)
- 1–3 tiers max. Perks that cost little time: early access, members-only community posts, monthly live Q&A, source files, badge/emojis, name in credits.
- Mention memberships in a natural moment, not every video.
- Typical: a small % of engaged viewers join. Memberships work best with strong community and live streams.

### 9. Communities (S4+)
- Free community (Discord) early for engagement → paid community (Skool, Circle, Discord with paid roles) once there's clear demand.
- A paid community needs ongoing value: accountability, feedback, events. It's a product, not a chat room.

### 10. Sponsorships (S3–S4+)
**When:** brands start reaching out around a few thousand subscribers in high-value niches; pitch proactively from ~5k.

**Media kit (1 page):** channel promise, audience (age, countries, interests), avg views per video (last 10, 30-day), engagement, past partners, formats & rates, contact.

**Pricing rule of thumb:** sponsored integrations are commonly priced as a **CPM on expected views** (often roughly $10–$50 per 1,000 views for an integration, higher in B2B/tech/finance). Start with a flat rate based on your average 30-day views and adjust as you learn.

**Formats:** 60–90s integration · dedicated video · Shorts package · newsletter placement · bundle.

**Protect trust:**
- Only products you'd recommend without payment.
- Integrate creatively, after the hook (never in the first 30 seconds).
- Tick "includes paid promotion"; disclose verbally.
- Get usage rights, deadlines, approval rounds and payment terms in writing.

**Outreach template:**
```
Subject: [Brand] × [Channel]: [specific idea]

Hi [name], I make videos for [viewer] about [topic]. ([X] avg views, [Y]% audience in [countries].)
I already use [product] for [use case], and my viewers keep asking about [problem it solves].
Idea: [specific video concept where the product fits naturally].
Media kit attached. Open to a quick chat?
```

### 11. Other streams
YouTube Shopping (own merch or affiliate products) · merch (only with a strong community identity) · licensing your footage · speaking/workshops · crowdfunding (Patreon) for creators whose audience values exclusive content · building a SaaS or tool for your audience (S5).

## C. The flywheel: how the streams feed each other

```
Video (free value)
  → affiliate link (at the moment of need)   → $ + data on what viewers buy
  → lead magnet → email list                 → product launches, sponsors, community
       → product buyers → testimonials/case studies → new videos
  → memberships/community                    → feedback → better videos
  → sponsors                                 → budget → better production → more viewers
```

## D. Revenue targets by stage (illustrative)

| Stage | Monthly revenue target | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | First $1–$100 | Affiliate + one small product/service |
| S2 | $100–$500 | + memberships |
| S3 | $500–$1,500 | + AdSense |
| S4 | $1,500–$10,000 | Sponsors + premium products > AdSense |
| S5 | $10,000+ | Course/community/business-led |

## E. Monthly money review (30 min)
- Revenue by stream vs last month
- **Revenue per 1,000 views (all streams)**: the most useful creator-business number
- Best-converting video for each stream → make more like it
- Email list growth and lead-magnet conversion rate
- One stream to improve, one to test next month

## F. Legal & admin basics
- Separate bank account for creator income; track expenses (gear, software, freelancers) for taxes.
- Read each platform's and affiliate program's disclosure rules and follow them every time.
- Contracts in writing for sponsors and clients.
- Check your local tax/business-registration requirements once income is regular.
