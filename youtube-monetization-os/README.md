# YouTube Monetization Operating System

A complete, repeatable system for taking a channel from **zero subscribers to the YouTube Partner Program (YPP)**, and building income that does not depend on AdSense alone.

> **How to use it:** Work through the modules in order. Module 01 is the only one that requires you to *think*; every other module turns your answers into rules, schedules and checklists. Re-run the diagnosis every 90 days.

---

## The finish line (verify current numbers in YouTube Studio → Earn)

| Tier | Subscribers | Plus ONE of | What unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Expanded YPP (fan funding)** | 500 | 3,000 public long-form watch hours (last 12 months) **or** 3M public Shorts views (last 90 days) — *and* 3 public uploads in last 90 days | Channel memberships, Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers, Shopping |
| **Full YPP (ad revenue)** | 1,000 | 4,000 public long-form watch hours (last 12 months) **or** 10M public Shorts views (last 90 days) | Watch-page ads + Shorts feed ad revenue share, everything above |

Also required: you live in an eligible country, you have 2-Step Verification on, no active Community Guidelines strikes, one linked AdSense account, and content that passes review. The review fails channels for **inauthentic (mass-produced or repetitive) content** and **reused content**, so original commentary, editing and value are not optional.

> Shorts watch time does **not** count toward the 4,000 hours. Long-form is the most reliable route to watch hours. Shorts are the most reliable way to find subscribers. This OS uses both.

## The 6-phase roadmap

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Diagnose** | 0 | Pick a niche you can win and sustain | Intake done, niche score ≥ 32/45, 30 video ideas banked |
| **1. Foundation** | 1–4 | Channel set up, workflow proven | 8 long-form + 12 Shorts published, packaging checklist in use |
| **2. Signal hunting** | 5–12 | Find the format/topic the algorithm rewards | 1+ video with CTR > 5% **and** AVD > 40%; 100 subs |
| **3. Double down** | 13–26 | Turn winners into series | Series #1 live; 500 subs; 1,500 watch hours |
| **4. Compounding** | 27–40 | Library + search traffic + collabs | Expanded YPP eligible; first non-AdSense revenue |
| **5. Monetize** | 41–52 | Cross 1,000 / 4,000 and stack revenue | Full YPP applied; 3 revenue streams live |

The timelines are achievable, not guaranteed. Most channels that fail don't fail on the timeline. They fail because they stop uploading in months 2–4, before signal arrives. The weekly system in module 08 is built to get you through that stretch.

## The 5 laws this system is built on

1. **Packaging before production.** If you can't write a title and thumbnail you'd click, don't film the video.
2. **One viewer, one promise.** Every video serves the same person with a version of the same promise.
3. **Volume finds signal; signal earns focus.** Publish enough to learn, then cut everything that isn't working.
4. **Retention is the product.** Views come from CTR × impressions; impressions come from satisfied viewers.
5. **Systems beat motivation.** Fixed days, fixed templates, fixed review. Willpower is not a strategy.
